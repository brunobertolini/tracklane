# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/),
version 3.0.

## In short

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not people. Harassment,
personal attacks and unwelcome attention are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **dev@brunobertolini.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban a
participant, proportionally to the incident.

The full text — including the enforcement ladder — is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/
